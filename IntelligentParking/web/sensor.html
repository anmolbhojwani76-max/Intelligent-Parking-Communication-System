One-time setup

  1. Point a fixed webcam at a paper or cardboard parking layout. Keep the camera and lighting steady.
  2. Align eight spaces with the displayed grid, or choose Draw custom zones and drag eight rectangles in A01–A08 order.
  3. Remove all objects, then choose Capture empty reference.
  4. Choose Start detection. Place a toy car or another object inside a marked space.
  5. The program detects sustained changes and transmits occupancy automatically. Keep this camera tab visible.

This detects visual occupancy / obstruction, not the identity of a car. Shadows, camera movement and major lighting changes can cause errors.

Camera frames stay in this browser. The sensor agent sends only eight Boolean occupancy readings.

Changing the zones requires a new empty reference. Closing or hiding the camera tab stops fresh camera reports.
